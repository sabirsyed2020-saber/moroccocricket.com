🇲🇺 Mauritius Becomes ICC's 111th Member

Published: July 2026

Mauritius has taken another important step on its cricket journey after being officially admitted as the 111th Member of the International Cricket Council (ICC).

The decision was approved by the ICC Board during its Annual Conference in Edinburgh on 15 July 2026. The admission makes Mauritius the latest country to join the global cricket family and highlights the continued expansion of the sport beyond its traditional strongholds.

A milestone for global cricket

Mauritius' admission reflects the growing reach of cricket and creates further opportunities for players, coaches, clubs and communities in the island nation to participate and develop within the international cricket structure.

The ICC said the decision was part of its continued effort to expand cricket's presence in emerging markets and create greater opportunities for participation and development around the world.

For cricket fans across Africa, the development is particularly significant. Mauritius joins a growing group of African cricket nations working to develop the sport and provide new opportunities for players and communities.

At Morocco Cricket, we believe stories such as this deserve attention. Cricket is not limited to the traditional cricket-playing nations. Its future can also be found in countries where the game is still developing and where new generations are discovering the sport.

Congratulations to Mauritius on this important milestone. We look forward to following the country's cricket journey and seeing what comes next.

Source: International Cricket Council (ICC)
